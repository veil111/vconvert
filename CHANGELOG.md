## v1.0.0 (2026-11-03)

First release.

- Download YouTube and SoundCloud links as audio, video, both, or just the thumbnail.
- Paste several links at once. Playlists and SoundCloud sets download every track.
- Audio as MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC or WAV. Video as MP4, MKV or WebM, up to 4K/8K.
- See the estimated size of every option before downloading.
- Optional thumbnail `.jpg` and info `.txt`.
- Checks for yt-dlp updates at start.
- Run it with `npm start`, or `npm link` once to use `vconverter` from anywhere.
